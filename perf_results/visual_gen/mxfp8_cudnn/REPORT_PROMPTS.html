TL;DR

MXFP8 is consistently 2-3× more accurate than Sage(1, 16, 1) across all 10 prompts at this small-S configuration (480×832 / 20 steps). MXFP8 mean LPIPS = 0.250 (noticeable on close inspection), Sage = 0.551 (visible difference). Single-prompt baselines like panda_bamboo (LPIPS ~0.15) under-estimate worst-case behavior — the 10-prompt mean is roughly 2× higher.

1. Setup & protocol

Three VisualGen processes spawned in parallel, one per GPU on umbriel-b200-027. Each process loads the model once and iterates through the 10-prompt suite, saving an mp4 + raw uint8 .npy per generation. Same seed (42), same resolution, same scheduler / step count, same prompt list across all three backends — apples-to-apples.

Per-call traces (TRTLLM_VISUAL_GEN_MXFP8_PER_CALL_TRACE and TRTLLM_VISUAL_GEN_SAGE_PER_CALL_TRACE) confirm both quantized backends actually fire on every main-run dispatch — see the Path firing row in the metadata above.

4. Worst-case & best-case analysis

Worst 3 prompts for MXFP8

rankpromptLPIPSfailure mode probed
1empty_room_sun0.404Minimal content, noise sensitivity
2busy_street0.356Many subjects, motion
3drone_city_night0.310Lighting, scene complexity

These point to MXFP8's weak spots: scenes with large flat areas (empty room, sky-dominated busy street, night sky with dim subjects) where small FP8 perturbations move many low-magnitude pixels across the round-to-uint8 boundary. Static-detail scenes (cat_windowsill) also score poorly because the absence of motion makes per-pixel noise unmasked.

6. Caveats & methodology

  • Resolution / step count: this study uses 480×832 / 9f / 20 steps. The prior single-prompt full-default 720×1280 / 81f / 40-step result (MXFP8 LPIPS 0.044) is much better. Two things compound to widen the gap here: (a) smaller S = less averaging of per-call FP8 noise, (b) fewer steps = less time for the diffusion to converge past the perturbation. A follow-up at 720×1280 / 40 steps on the top-3 failure prompts would tighten the verdict.
  • Sage (1, 16, 1) at small S is a known weak point — the PR's heuristic in visual_gen_wan_t2v.py says to use (1, 4, 1) for the small 1.3B Wan model. We measured (1, 16, 1) here only because the 14B A14B is what the heuristic recommends for, but the small-resolution config we used pushes it past its design point. Cross-resolution discussion in REPORT_SAGE.
  • All metrics are computed at uint8 video level after postprocess_video_tensor's clamp+round. A pre-uint8 fp16 latent comparison would separate FP8 attention error from VAE rounding artifacts.
  • Single seed (42) per prompt. Multi-seed averaging (3+ seeds per prompt) would tighten LPIPS bands and confirm the trend.
  • VANILLA → VANILLA sanity not measured here (trivially 0 since seed is fixed); confirmed in prior studies.
  • LPIPS metric assumes natural-image priors (AlexNet trained on ImageNet). For very synthetic content like the text_hello prompt the LPIPS may under-estimate human-visible quality differences vs PSNR/SSIM.
How to reproduce 
# Container: trtllm-mxfp8-liuc:027 on umbriel-b200-027 + Sage overlay
# Same prompts and configuration from this run.

docker exec tensorrt_llm-jenkins-liuc bash -lc 'nohup bash -c "
MODEL=/home/liuc/scratch/Wan2.2-T2V-A14B-Diffusers
OUT=/home/liuc/scratch/codes/trtllm-v3-wt-2/perf_results/visual_gen/mxfp8_cudnn
cd /tmp
for cmd in \
  "--backend VANILLA --backend_tag VANILLA --gpu_id 0" \
  "--backend MXFP8_CUDNN --backend_tag MXFP8 --gpu_id 1" \
  "--backend TRTLLM --backend_tag sage_blk16 --sage_blk_k 16 --gpu_id 2"; do
  HOME=/tmp python3 /code/tensorrt_llm/perf_bench/visual_gen/mxfp8_cudnn/run_prompt_suite.py \
    $cmd --model_path $MODEL --out_dir $OUT &
done; wait
HOME=/tmp CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 python3 \
  /code/tensorrt_llm/perf_bench/visual_gen/mxfp8_cudnn/compute_prompt_metrics.py
" &'
